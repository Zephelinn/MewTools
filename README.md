## Introduction

MewTools is an all-in-one (AIO) toolset dedicated to helping you manage and utilize your DMA hardware.

Supports **35T, 75T, and 100T** cards

## Features

### 🔢 Hardware Auto-Detect

Automatically detect which Artix-7 chip is being utilized within your DMA card.

### 🔢 DNA Grabber

Fetch the DNA ID of your DMA card to provide to firmware developers.

### 🔢 Speed Test

Run a speed test to see how fast your DMA card really is!

### 🔢 Driver Installation

Easily install drivers required to manage and use your DMA card and Makcu device.

### 🔢 Device Flasher

Easily flash your DMA card with any firmware .bin you have available.

### 🔢 Makcu Management

Manage your Makcu mouse emulation device with ease.
